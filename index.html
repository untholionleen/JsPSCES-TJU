Thermodynamic Equations & Logic

Soave-Redlich-Kwong (SRK) equation

$$P = \frac{RT}{V - b} - \frac{a(T)}{V(V + b)}$$
$$a(T) = 0.42747 \frac{R^2 T_c^2}{P_c} \left[1 + m \left(1 - \sqrt{T_r}\right)\right]^2$$

SRK improves upon RK by replacing the temperature term with a generalized acentric factor dependency, greatly enhancing vapor pressure predictions.

Peng-Robinson (PR) equation

$$P = \frac{RT}{V - b} - \frac{a(T)}{V(V + b) + b(V - b)}$$

The cubic roots are resolved using analytical Cardano's method over the general form: $$Z^3 + c_2Z^2 + c_1Z + c_0 = 0$$

Highest root = Vapour compressibility ($Z^V$), Lowest root = Liquid compressibility ($Z^L$).
