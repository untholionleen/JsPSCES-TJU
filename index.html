Van der Waals equation (1873)

$$P = \frac{RT}{V - b} - \frac{a}{V^2}$$
$$a = \frac{27 R^2 T_c^2}{64 P_c}$$
$$b = \frac{R T_c}{8 P_c}$$

First EOS describing fluid properties over wide pressure range.

Redlich-Kwong equation (1949)

$$P = \frac{RT}{V - b} - \frac{a}{T^{0.5} V (V + b)}$$

Soave-Redlich-Kwong equation (1972)

$$P = \frac{RT}{V - b} - \frac{a(T)}{V(V + b)}$$

Peng-Robinson equation (1976)

$$P = \frac{RT}{V - b} - \frac{a(T)}{V(V + b) + b(V - b)}$$

Peng-Robinson-Gasem equation

Modified alpha formulation for heavy hydrocarbons.

Critical Parameters Database

16 standard pure substances loaded with true thermodynamic critical temperature and pressure values.

Solving Cubic Equations

Cardano's method used to yield 1 or 3 real roots for compressibility factor Z.

Advanced Thermodynamic Functions

Calculates fugacity coefficients and departure functions for enthalpy and entropy.

Mixing Rules for Binary Mixtures

Standard van der Waals one-fluid mixing rules applied with k12.

VLE Calculation Types

Bubble point, dew point, and isothermal flash calculations supported.

Notation Method

Definitions of mole fractions and departure properties.
